## Persistent motion and overlay rules

1. Camera translation, zoom, or pitch remains non-zero across any `0.5s` window.
2. After landing (~`f0009+`), the top-center source watermark stays dead still.
3. Caption chips use a dark rounded rectangle, white sentence-case type, lower ~72–78% of frame height, and hard-cut phrase switches (no karaoke word-pop).
4. Country fills stay semi-transparent with a white exterior stroke so terrain remains readable.
5. Yellow country chips frequently sit on landmass interiors (Geoverum maps the same mechanic to open-water / margin callouts).
